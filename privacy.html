FlyKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

FlyKey is a macOS touch typing trainer. The Mac App Store version is designed to work locally on your Mac. It does not require an account, does not include third-party analytics, and does not send your typing practice, progress, settings, or custom text to FlyKey servers.

Data the macOS app stores locally

FlyKey may store your selected language, theme, text size, sound setting, finger map, lesson progress, practice statistics, and other app preferences locally on your device. This local data is used only to make the app remember your settings and progress between sessions.

Data collection

The Mac App Store version of FlyKey does not collect personal data from you and does not transmit app usage data to FlyKey or third-party analytics services.

Accounts and cloud sync

The current Mac App Store release is local-first. Account and cloud sync features are disabled unless a production backend is explicitly configured in a future release. If cloud features are added later, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before release.

Website analytics

The FlyKey website may offer optional analytics with consent. This does not apply to the Mac App Store app, where analytics is disabled.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@flykey.org.
