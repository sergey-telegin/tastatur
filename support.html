FlyKey Support

FlyKey is a macOS touch typing trainer for Russian, Ukrainian, Kazakh, German, and English keyboard layouts.

Contact

For support, feedback, or privacy questions, email support@flykey.org.

Before contacting support

  • Check that you are using the latest App Store version.
  • Include your macOS version and FlyKey app version.
  • If the issue is about a lesson, include the language and lesson number.
